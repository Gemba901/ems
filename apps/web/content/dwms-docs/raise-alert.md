# Raise an Alert

Select **Raise New Alert** from Alerts or choose **Alert** in the new action page. Enter a clear title, description, and severity, then choose a target.

- **Person:** The selected employee is responsible and must acknowledge each raise.
- **Task:** The task owner is responsible. The original task assigner can raise an alert for that task even when the owner is outside their reporting team.
- **Department:** No individual is responsible or required to acknowledge.
- **General:** An organization alert available to HOD and Management. No individual acknowledgment is required.

When you select a person or task, previous alert histories for that person appear in the form. Choose **Create a new alert** for a separate issue, or select a previous history and **Raise Previous Alert Again**. Re-raising keeps its original details and adds a timestamped occurrence with your name. The count increases; on the third raise the same history appears under Abnormalities.

The responsible person acknowledges each occurrence on the alert detail page with a written note. Everyone with access to the history can read the timeline and add ordinary comments.
