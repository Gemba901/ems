# DWMS Settings

DWMS Settings controls task approver rules, alert visibility, and analytics visibility. Management, Admin, HR, and Super Admin roles can update these settings.

Choose the roles eligible to approve task completion. The task owner is always eligible, **Anyone** is exclusive, and **Custom** supports up to three employees from the organization.

Alert visibility controls department- and organization-targeted alert views. Personal alerts remain visible to the responsible employee, while reporting managers can view alerts for their direct and indirect reportees. Department visibility does not expose person-targeted alerts for everyone in that department.

Analytics visibility controls personal, department, and organization dashboards and their permitted employee drilldowns. HOD users always have at least Department visibility; Management, Admin, HR, and Super Admin users always have Organization visibility.
