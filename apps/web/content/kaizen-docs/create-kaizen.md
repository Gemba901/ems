# Create a kaizen

New kaizens are created with a four-step wizard. You can stop at any time. Your work is saved as a draft, and nothing is sent to your HOD until you submit.

> You do not need every answer at once. Fill in what you know, select **Save draft**, and come back later.

## 1. Start a new kaizen

Select **New Kaizen** in the Kaizen sidebar or on the Overview page. A draft is created and step 1 opens. You are set as the kaizen owner and today as the start date; you can change both in step 3.

To continue a draft later, open **Overview**, find the kaizen under **My Kaizens**, and select **Continue draft**.

## 2. Step 1: Problem

1. **Why did you start this kaizen?** Choose the closest reason from the grouped list. If none fits, choose **Other** and explain.
2. **Short title:** A few words someone can recognise in a list, for example "Label the spill kit".
3. **What is the problem or idea?** Say what you saw, where, and why it matters. Tap the microphone to speak instead of typing.
4. **Photos or files (optional):** Add up to 8 photos or files of how things are today.
5. **Linked to an alert, audit or other record? (optional):** Open this if the kaizen comes from an alert, an abnormality, an audit finding, or another record, and enter its reference.

## 3. Step 2: What will improve

Choose at least one area that the kaizen will improve and say how you will measure it:

- **Area:** Quality, Cost, Delivery, Safety, Morale, or Technology (QCDSMT). Each button shows a short hint.
- **What will you measure?** For example "Time to find the spill kit".
- **Now:** The value today.
- **Goal:** The value you expect after the change.
- **Unit:** For example minutes, pieces, percent, or currency.

**Does it cut any waste?** is optional. Open it to record which of the seven wastes the kaizen reduces.

## 4. Step 3: Owner & plan

- **Start date** and **Finish by:** A daily kaizen should be finished within 30 days of the start date.
- **Kaizen owner:** The person who does the work and reports the result. Usually you.
- **Team members (optional):** Up to three colleagues from your department.
- **What do you need to do it?** Materials, tools, or help. Write "Nothing" if you need nothing.
- **Estimated cost (optional):** Leave it blank if the change costs nothing or you do not know yet. The currency you choose is remembered for next time.

## 5. Step 4: Review & submit

The **Before you submit** list shows anything still missing. Select an item to jump to the step where you can fix it. **Submit for HOD review** becomes available when the list is complete.

Required before submitting:

- A reason, and an explanation when the reason is Other.
- A title and a description of what you saw.
- At least one improvement area.
- A start date, a finish-by date, and a kaizen owner.
- What you need to do it.

## 6. Delete a draft

If you started a kaizen by mistake, open step 4 and select **Delete draft**, then confirm. Only drafts that have never been submitted can be deleted.

## 7. When the kaizen is returned

If your HOD returns the kaizen, it shows **Returned for Revision** with the HOD's remarks in the **What's next** card. Open it, update the steps, and submit again.
