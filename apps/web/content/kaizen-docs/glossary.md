# Glossary

## Kaizen

A Japanese word meaning "change for the better". A Daily Kaizen is a small, quick improvement made by the people who do the work.

## QCDSMT

The six areas a kaizen can improve:

- **Quality:** Defects, rework, complaints.
- **Cost:** Money, material, energy.
- **Delivery:** Lead time, delays, output.
- **Safety:** Injuries, near misses, environment.
- **Morale:** Teamwork, workload, attendance.
- **Technology:** Automation, systems, data.

## The seven wastes

Effort that adds no value for the customer:

- **Transportation:** Moving materials or products more than needed.
- **Inventory:** Stock or work waiting to be used.
- **Motion:** People walking, reaching, or searching.
- **Waiting:** People or machines idle.
- **Overproduction:** Making more, or sooner, than needed.
- **Overprocessing:** Extra steps the customer does not need.
- **Defects:** Scrap, rework, and mistakes.

## Kaizen owner

The person who carries out the change, records the result, and submits it for verification.

## SGA

Small Group Activity. A team project for problems too big for a single kaizen. An HOD can move a kaizen to an SGA.
