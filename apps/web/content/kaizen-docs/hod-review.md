# HOD review

Before any work starts, the HOD of the raiser's department reviews each submitted kaizen.

## 1. Find kaizens waiting for you

Kaizens waiting for your review show **Pending HOD Pre-Review**. Open the **Department** tab on the Overview page to see your department's kaizens. The **What's next** card on a kaizen says **Your turn** when it is waiting for you.

## 2. Make a decision

Open the kaizen, read steps 1 to 3, and choose one of these in the **HOD Pre-Implementation Review** section:

- **Approve:** The owner can start the work. The status becomes **In Implementation**.
- **Return:** The raiser needs to change something. Write clear remarks; the raiser sees them and submits again.
- **Reject:** The kaizen will not go ahead. Explain why in the remarks.
- **Move to SGA:** The problem is too big for one person and needs a team. The status becomes **Moved to SGA**. The kaizen is not converted automatically, so the raiser or the team should start a new SGA for it.

The raiser is notified of your decision.
