# Overview and All Kaizens

## 1. Overview

The Overview page shows four numbers and your lists:

- **My Kaizens:** Kaizens you raised.
- **In Implementation:** Your kaizens that are being carried out.
- **Pending My Review:** Kaizens waiting for your verification.
- **Verified This Month:** Kaizens closed this month.

Use the tabs to switch between **My Kaizens**, your **Department**, and kaizens waiting for your **Verification**. Search by title and filter by status.

Your own drafts show **Continue draft**, and kaizens returned to you show **Update & resubmit**. Select the row to pick up where you left off.

## 2. All Kaizens

Administrators and management see **All Kaizens** in the sidebar. It lists every kaizen in the organization, with filters for status and department.
