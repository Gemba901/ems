# What is a Daily Kaizen?

A Daily Kaizen is a small improvement that one person can make in their own area, usually within days. You spot a problem or an idea, say what will get better, make the change, and show the result.

> Use a Daily Kaizen for a quick fix that you can do yourself or with one to three colleagues. When a problem needs a team, meetings, and a root-cause analysis, use a Small Group Activity (SGA) instead.

## 1. When to raise a kaizen

Raise a kaizen when:

- You notice a problem or a better way of working in your own area.
- You can make the change within 30 days.
- You can show what changed, for example with a before and after photo or a number.

## 2. The five stages

The progress bar at the top of each kaizen shows where it is.

1. **Create:** The raiser explains what they saw, what will improve, who owns it, and what is needed.
2. **HOD review:** The department HOD approves the kaizen before work starts, sends it back, rejects it, or moves it to an SGA.
3. **Implement:** The kaizen owner makes the change and records what was done, with after photos and the result.
4. **Verify:** Reviewers confirm the result.
5. **Close:** When every applicable review is verified, the kaizen is closed.

## 3. Statuses

- **Draft:** Being written by the raiser. Only the raiser can edit it.
- **Pending HOD Pre-Review:** Submitted and waiting for the department HOD.
- **Returned for Revision:** The HOD sent it back. The raiser updates it and submits again.
- **Rejected:** The HOD decided not to go ahead.
- **Moved to SGA:** The HOD decided the problem needs a team, so it should be handled as an SGA.
- **In Implementation:** Approved. The kaizen owner is making the change.
- **Pending Verification:** The owner has submitted the result and it is waiting for the reviewers.
- **Returned for Rework:** A reviewer asked for more work before closing.
- **Verified & Closed:** Every applicable review has verified the result.

## 4. Who does what

- **Raiser:** Creates the kaizen and submits it. The raiser can delete their own draft before it is submitted.
- **Kaizen owner:** Does the work, records the implementation, and submits for verification. New kaizens are owned by the raiser unless they pick someone else.
- **Team members:** Up to three colleagues who help. They are notified when they are added.
- **Department HOD:** Reviews the kaizen before work starts and completes the HOD verification.
- **Steering committee:** Confirms the result for the organization.
- **Finance HOD:** Confirms the money saved when the kaizen targets Cost.

## 5. Where to find what you need

The **What's next** card on each kaizen says what happens next and who it is waiting on. If it says **Your turn**, the next action is yours.
