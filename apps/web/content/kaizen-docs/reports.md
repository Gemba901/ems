# Reports

The Reports page shows how many kaizens are raised, where they are, and how many get closed.

## 1. Choose what to look at

- **Scope:** Administrators and management see the whole **Organisation**. HODs see **My department**. Everyone sees **My Kaizens**.
- **Period:** The last 90 days, the last 12 months, or all time. The period counts kaizens by the date they were created.

## 2. The numbers

- **Submitted:** Kaizens that have left draft. The number of drafts is shown underneath.
- **Active:** Waiting for HOD review, returned, being implemented, or in verification.
- **Closed:** Verified and closed.
- **Closure rate:** Closed kaizens as a share of submitted ones.
- **Avg. time to close:** Average days from creation to closure.
- **Overdue:** Active kaizens past their finish-by date. The number moved to SGA is shown underneath.

## 3. The charts

- **Raised vs closed:** Submitted and closed kaizens per month for the last six months.
- **Where kaizens are now:** How many kaizens are at each status.
- **What people are improving:** How many kaizens target each QCDSMT area.
- **Top reasons for starting:** The five most common reasons.
- **By department:** Submitted, active, closed, and overdue kaizens for each department. Not shown for My Kaizens.
