# Verify and close

Reviewers confirm the result before a kaizen is closed.

## 1. Verification stages

- **HOD:** The department HOD confirms the change was made and works.
- **Steering committee:** A steering committee member confirms the result for the organization.
- **Finance:** The Finance HOD confirms the money benefit. This stage only applies when the kaizen targets **Cost**; otherwise it is marked not applicable.

The department HOD can also collect the committee or Finance decision and record it on their behalf. The kaizen shows when a decision was recorded this way.

## 2. Record your decision

Open the kaizen, check the implementation and after photos, and in your stage choose:

- **Verify:** The result is confirmed. Finance can also record the verified benefit amount.
- **Return:** More work is needed. Write remarks so the owner knows what to fix.

## 3. What happens next

- When every applicable stage is verified, the kaizen becomes **Verified & Closed**.
- If any stage returns it, the kaizen becomes **Returned for Rework** and every stage must verify again after the owner resubmits.
