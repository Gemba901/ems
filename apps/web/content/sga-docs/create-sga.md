# Create an SGA

New SGAs are created with a four-step wizard. You can stop at any time; your work is saved as a draft and nothing is sent to your HOD until you submit.

> You do not need every answer on the first day. Fill in what you know, select **Save draft**, and come back later.

## 1. Start a new SGA

Select **New SGA** in the SGA sidebar or on the Overview page. A draft is created and step 1 of the wizard opens.

To continue a draft later, open **Overview**, find the SGA under **My SGAs**, and select **Continue draft**.

## 2. Step 1: Problem

Answer the questions in this order:

1. **Reason:** Why are you starting this SGA? Choose the closest reason from the grouped list. If none fits, choose **Other** and describe it.
2. **Linked reference (optional):** If the SGA comes from an audit, SOP, customer specification, or similar document, record its type and number.
3. **SGA information and problem:** Describe what happens, where, and how often. Tap the microphone to speak instead of typing. Add a short title, the main department, the line or area, the start and target dates, and photos of the problem as it is today.

The main department decides which HOD approves the SGA. It is filled in with your own department, and you can change it.

## 3. Step 2: What will improve

Choose at least one area that the SGA will improve and say how you will measure it:

- **Area:** Quality, Cost, Delivery, Safety, Morale, or Technology (QCDSMT). A row is added for you based on the reason you chose.
- **What is measured:** For example "Bottles rejected per shift".
- **Now:** The value today.
- **Goal:** The value you want to reach.
- **Unit:** For example pieces, minutes, percent, or currency.

Open **More details** to add the expected benefit for a row. The waste section is optional; use it when the problem is one of the seven wastes.

## 4. Step 3: Team & meetings

- **SGA leader:** The person who runs meetings and owns the actions. This is required before you submit.
- **Team members:** People who will work on the SGA. You can add people from other departments when they are involved.
- **Team meetings:** How often the team meets, and optionally the day, time, and place. Weekly is selected by default.
- **Resources & cost:** Anything the team needs, and the approximate cost. Leave the amount blank if there is no cost or it is not known yet. A currency is only needed when you enter an amount.

## 5. Step 4: Review & submit

The **Before you submit** list shows anything still missing. Select an item to jump to the step where you can fix it. **Submit for HOD approval** becomes available when the list is complete.

Required before submitting:

- A reason, and a description when the reason is Other.
- A title and a problem description.
- The main department and the start and target dates.
- At least one improvement area.
- An SGA leader and a meeting frequency.

## 6. Delete a draft

If you started an SGA by mistake, open step 4 and select **Delete draft**, then confirm. Only drafts that have never been submitted can be deleted.

## 7. When the SGA is returned

If your HOD returns the SGA, it shows **Returned for Revision** with the HOD's remarks at the top. Open it, update the steps, and submit again.
