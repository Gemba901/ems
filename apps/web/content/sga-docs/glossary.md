# Glossary

## QCDSMT

The six areas an SGA can improve:

- **Quality:** Defects, rework, complaints.
- **Cost:** Money, material, energy.
- **Delivery:** Lead time, delays, output.
- **Safety:** Injuries, near misses, environment.
- **Morale:** Teamwork, difficulty of work, engagement.
- **Technology:** Automation, systems, data.

## The seven wastes

Activities that use time or resources without adding value:

- **Transportation:** Moving material more than needed.
- **Inventory:** Holding more stock or work in progress than needed.
- **Motion:** Unnecessary movement of people.
- **Waiting:** People or machines idle while waiting.
- **Overproduction:** Making more, or earlier, than needed.
- **Overprocessing:** Doing more work than the customer needs.
- **Defects:** Scrap, rework, and errors.

## Other terms

- **Baseline:** The value of a measure before the improvement ("Now").
- **Target:** The value the team wants to reach ("Goal").
- **Fishbone (5M):** A diagram that groups possible causes under People, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement, and Environment.
- **Why-Why analysis:** Asking "why?" repeatedly until you reach a root cause you can act on.
- **Pareto analysis:** Ranking causes by how often they occur to find the few that matter most.
- **SOP:** Standard Operating Procedure.
- **SMED:** Single-Minute Exchange of Die, a method for reducing changeover time.
- **WIP:** Work in progress.
- **Steering committee:** The group that oversees continuous improvement in the organization.
