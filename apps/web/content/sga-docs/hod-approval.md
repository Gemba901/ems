# HOD approval

When an SGA is submitted, the HOD of its main department receives a notification. The HOD decides whether the team should go ahead.

> Admin and Management users can also record the approval decision.

## 1. Find SGAs waiting for you

Open **Overview** and select the **My Department** tab, or open the notification. Admin and Management users can use **All SGAs**. SGAs waiting for you show **Pending HOD Approval**.

## 2. Review the SGA

Check stages 1 and 2:

- Is the problem real, clear, and measurable?
- Are the improvement targets realistic?
- Is the right team involved, including other departments where needed?
- Are the resources and cost acceptable?

## 3. Record your decision

In the **HOD Approval** section, choose one decision and add remarks:

- **Approve:** The SGA moves to **In Progress** and the team can start stage 3.
- **Return:** The SGA moves to **Returned for Revision**. Explain what needs to change; the raiser sees your remarks at the top of the SGA.
- **Reject:** The SGA moves to **Rejected** and stops. Explain why.

The raiser and the SGA leader are notified of your decision.
