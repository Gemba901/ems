# Overview and All SGAs

## 1. Overview

**Overview** is the SGA home page. The cards at the top show:

- **My SGAs:** SGAs you raised, lead, or are a team member of.
- **In Progress:** Your SGAs that are being worked on.
- **Pending My Review:** SGAs waiting for your verification decision.
- **Verified This Month:** Your SGAs closed this month.

## 2. Tabs

- **My SGAs:** Everything you are involved in.
- **My Department:** SGAs in your department. Available to users with a department; Admin and Management users use **All SGAs** instead.
- **Pending My Review:** SGAs waiting for your verification decision.

Use the search box, status filter, and department filter to narrow the list.

Your own drafts show **Continue draft**, and SGAs returned to you show **Update & resubmit**. Select the row to continue.

## 3. All SGAs

**All SGAs** lists every SGA in the organization. It is available to Admin and Management users.
