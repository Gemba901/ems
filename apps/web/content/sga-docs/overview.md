# What is an SGA?

A Small Group Activity (SGA) is a short improvement project in which a small team works on one real problem in its own area. The team measures how things are today, finds the root cause, fixes it, and proves that the fix worked.

> Use an SGA when a problem keeps coming back and needs a team to solve it. For a quick one-person idea, use the suggestion system instead.

## 1. When to start an SGA

Start an SGA when:

- The same defect, delay, breakdown, or safety issue happens again and again.
- You can measure the problem, for example scrap per shift, minutes lost, or cost per month.
- Fixing it needs more than one person or more than one department.

## 2. The six stages

Every SGA moves through the same six stages. The progress bar at the top of an SGA shows where it is.

1. **Start:** The raiser explains why the SGA is needed, what the problem is, and what will improve.
2. **Team & Approve:** The raiser picks the SGA leader, team, meeting plan, and resources, then submits the SGA. The HOD of the main department approves, returns, or rejects it.
3. **Understand & Analyse:** The team records the current condition, holds meetings, and finds the root cause.
4. **Plan & Implement:** The team confirms the causes, agrees actions with owners and dates, and records what was implemented.
5. **Check Results:** The team records the final result against each baseline and target, together with the benefits and how the change will be sustained.
6. **Verify & Close:** Reviewers confirm the results. When every applicable review is verified, the SGA is closed.

## 3. Statuses

- **Draft:** Being written by the raiser. Only the raiser can see and edit it.
- **Pending HOD Approval:** Submitted and waiting for the main department HOD.
- **Returned for Revision:** The HOD sent it back. The raiser updates it and submits again.
- **Rejected:** The HOD decided not to continue with this SGA.
- **In Progress:** Approved. The team is working through stages 3 to 5.
- **Pending Verification:** Submitted for closure and waiting for the reviewers.
- **Returned for Rework:** A reviewer asked for more work before closing.
- **Verified & Closed:** Every applicable review has verified the result.

## 4. Who does what

- **Raiser:** Creates the SGA, completes stages 1 and 2, and submits it. The raiser can delete their own draft before it is submitted.
- **SGA leader:** Runs the meetings, owns the actions, and is the only person who can record implementation details.
- **Team members:** Can update stages 3 to 5 while the SGA is In Progress or Returned for Rework.
- **HOD of the main department:** Approves the SGA and completes the HOD verification.
- **Verifying department HOD or representative:** Confirms the result when another department is affected.
- **Steering committee:** Confirms the result for the organization.
- **Finance HOD:** Confirms the money benefit when the SGA targets Cost.

## 5. Where to find what you need

The banner at the top of each SGA tells you what happens next and who it is waiting on. If it says **Your turn**, the next action is yours.
