# Reports

Reports show how many SGAs are being raised, where they are in the process, and how many are closed.

> Reports help you spot a pattern. Open the SGAs themselves to understand the cause.

## 1. Open Reports

Select **Reports** in the SGA sidebar.

## 2. Choose what to look at

- **Organisation:** Every SGA in the organization. Available to Admin and Management users.
- **My department:** SGAs in your department. Available to HODs.
- **My SGAs:** SGAs you raised, lead, or are a team member of. Available to everyone.

Use the period buttons to show SGAs created in the last 90 days, the last 12 months, or all time.

## 3. Understand the numbers

- **Submitted:** SGAs that have left the draft stage. The detail line shows how many are still drafts.
- **Active:** SGAs pending HOD approval, returned for revision, in progress, pending verification, or returned for rework.
- **Closed:** SGAs that are Verified & Closed.
- **Closure rate:** Closed divided by submitted.
- **Avg. time to close:** Average days from creation to closure, for closed SGAs.
- **Overdue:** Active SGAs whose target completion date has passed.

An em dash (—) means there is not enough data to calculate the number.

## 4. Charts

- **Raised vs closed:** Submitted and closed SGAs for each of the last six months.
- **Where SGAs are now:** How many SGAs are at each status. A long bar at one status can show where SGAs are getting stuck.
- **What teams are improving:** How many SGAs target each QCDSMT area.
- **Top reasons for starting:** The five most common reasons.
- **By department:** Submitted, active, closed, and overdue SGAs for each main department. Not shown for My SGAs.

## 5. What to look for

- **Many drafts, few submitted:** Raisers may need help completing the wizard.
- **Many pending HOD approval:** Approvals may be slow.
- **Many overdue:** Check target dates and whether the teams need support.
- **Low closure rate:** Check whether SGAs are waiting in verification.
