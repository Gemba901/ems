# Run the SGA

After HOD approval the SGA is **In Progress**. The raiser, the SGA leader, and team members work on it over the following weeks. You don't need to fill it in all at once.

The approved plan folds into one line, **Plan & HOD approval**, at the top. Open it at any time to check what was agreed.

Below it, the SGA has three tabs:

- **Stages:** the work itself, one stage at a time.
- **Meetings:** the team's meeting notes.
- **Actions:** a checklist of every agreed action.

Use **Add meeting note** at the top at any time, from any tab.

## How stages open

Only the current stage is open. Stages you have finished fold down to a one-line summary. You can still open and change them.

Later stages stay locked until the stage before has its minimum content. Each locked stage says what it is waiting for:

| Stage | Opens when |
| --- | --- |
| 3. Understand & Analyse | Straight after HOD approval |
| 4. Plan & Implement | Stage 3 has a baseline measure and a root cause |
| 5. Check Results | Stage 4 has an action and the implementation summary |
| 6. Verify & Close | Stage 5 has at least one final result |

> Every section has its own **Save** button. Save each section when you finish it. Opening another stage keeps your unsaved changes, but leaving the page does not.

## Stage 3: Understand & Analyse

**Current condition.** Describe the situation as it is today and where the evidence came from. Tick **Immediate control needed?** if something must be contained now, before the root cause is fixed.

Add **baseline measures**: what is measured, today's value, and the target. These are the numbers you will check against in stage 5.

**Root cause analysis.** Choose one or more tools:

- **Fishbone (5M):** List possible causes under People, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement, and Environment.
- **Why-Why analysis:** Ask "why?" repeatedly until you reach a cause you can act on.
- **Pareto analysis, process observation, or data trend analysis:** Summarise your findings and attach files.

## Stage 4: Plan & Implement

**Confirmed causes and action plan.** For each confirmed cause, agree an action, the person responsible, and a due date.

**Implementation.** The SGA leader records what was implemented, the actual cost, the implementation status, and photos after the change. Only the SGA leader can edit this section.

## Stage 5: Check Results

Record the final value for each baseline measure. Then record the benefit achieved and the period it applies to, for example per month.

Confirm how the change will last:

- The SOP was updated.
- Employees were trained.
- A follow-up check is planned.
- The solution was applied in other areas.

Add lessons learned so other teams can benefit.

## Stage 6: Verify & Close

If another department is affected by the change, select it and, optionally, a representative who will verify the result.

The **Ready to submit?** checklist shows which stages are complete. Once stages 3 to 5 are complete, the raiser or the SGA leader selects **Submit for Verification**.

## Meetings tab

Record each team meeting: the date, who attended, and what was discussed. The tab also shows the meeting plan agreed at the start, for example "weekly on Monday at 10:00". Regular notes show the SGA is moving.

## Actions tab

Every action from stage 4 appears here as a checklist:

- Tap the circle to mark an action done, or switch it between **To do**, **Doing**, and **Done**.
- Overdue actions are outlined in red and listed first.
- Use **Mine** to see only the actions assigned to you, and **Overdue** to see what is late.

Team members can update any action. The person responsible for an action can always update it, even if they are not in the team.
