# Run the SGA

After HOD approval the SGA is **In Progress**. The raiser, the SGA leader, and team members can now complete stages 3 to 5.

> Work through the sections in order. Each one builds on the one before.

## 1. Meeting reports

Record each team meeting: the date, who attended, what was discussed, and the agreed next steps. Regular meeting reports show the SGA is moving.

## 2. Current condition

Describe the situation as it is today and where the evidence came from. Tick **Immediate control needed?** if something must be contained now, before the root cause is fixed.

Add **baseline measures**: what is measured, today's value, and the target. These are the numbers you will check against in stage 5.

## 3. Root cause analysis

Choose one or more tools:

- **Fishbone (5M):** List possible causes under People, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement, and Environment.
- **Why-Why analysis:** Ask "why?" repeatedly until you reach a cause you can act on.
- **Pareto analysis, process observation, or data trend analysis:** Summarise your findings and attach files.

## 4. Confirmed causes and action plan

For each confirmed cause, agree an action, the person responsible, and a due date. Update the status of each action as work progresses.

## 5. Implementation

The SGA leader records what was implemented, the actual cost, the implementation status, and photos after the change. Only the SGA leader can edit this section.

## 6. Check the results

Record the final value for each baseline measure and whether the target was met.

## 7. Benefits and sustainability

Record the benefit achieved and the period it applies to, for example per month. Confirm how the change will last:

- The SOP was updated.
- Employees were trained.
- A follow-up check is planned.
- The solution was applied in other areas.

Add lessons learned so other teams can benefit.

## 8. Choose a verifying department

If another department is affected by the change, select it and, optionally, a representative who will verify the result.
