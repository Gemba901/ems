# Verify and close

When the results are recorded, the raiser or SGA leader selects **Submit for Verification**. The SGA moves to **Pending Verification** and every reviewer is notified.

> The implementation summary must be completed before the SGA can be submitted for verification.

## 1. Verification stages

- **Affected Department:** Applies when a verifying department was selected. Completed by that department's HOD or the chosen representative.
- **Department HOD:** Always applies. Completed by the HOD of the main department.
- **Steering Committee:** Always applies. Completed by a steering committee member.
- **Finance:** Applies when the SGA targets Cost. Completed by the Finance HOD.

Stages that do not apply are shown as **Not applicable**. The main department HOD and Admin users can complete any stage when needed.

## 2. Record a decision

Open the SGA, go to **Verification & Closure**, and for your stage choose:

- **Verified:** The result is confirmed.
- **Return:** More work is needed. Add remarks explaining what to fix.

## 3. What happens next

- When every applicable stage is **Verified**, the SGA becomes **Verified & Closed**.
- If any stage is **Returned**, the SGA becomes **Returned for Rework** and all stages go back to **Pending**. The team updates stages 3 to 5 and submits for verification again.

The raiser and the SGA leader are notified of each outcome.
