Area 01 · Strategic Direction

Write your company direction.

Enter your purpose, vision, mission and values below. You can save your work at any stage.

How to use this section

What is Strategic Direction? It is the organisation’s shared understanding of why it exists, the future it wants and how its people should work.

Why it matters: Clear direction gives management and employees a common starting point for decisions and performance planning.

Who uses it? Leadership prepares the wording. One authorised person can finalise it. Employees read the approved version.

How to work: Enter your company details, purpose, vision, mission and values. Explain the meaning, not just the wording. Save an incomplete draft at any time. Review the six fixed pillars, then preview the complete direction.

Simple approval: Management can finalise directly. An editor can send a completed draft to the same single approver. A revision keeps the existing approved version visible until a replacement is approved.

What happens next? Area 02 uses EMS organisation records to establish structure and responsibility. Area 03 creates objectives and goals. Use the top tabs to open Area 02 or Area 03. Your company direction is reused, not entered again.

Prototype only: Switch demonstration views to test the workflow; this is not a secure login. Changes use the selected demo identity. Dates are shown in East Africa Time (EAT). Keep real employee or confidential company information out of this file. Save in this browser and download a backup before moving devices.

01.02 · Fixed strategic perspectives
05

Our six excellence pillars

Six fixed perspectives for objective planning in Area 03. Select Know more to understand each one.

How to use the six pillars

Read what each pillar covers. These are strategic perspectives, not departments or a required number of objectives. The six names and codes stay fixed. In Area 03, leadership will select the main pillar for each objective. There is no separate objective register on this page.

01.03 · Preview & review
06

Preview & finalise

Read the whole direction together before making it the approved version.

How review and revisions work

Save draft keeps incomplete work. Preview shows the text as a reading page. Management can use Finalise & lock when the required information is complete; an editor sends the draft for one review. Approved wording cannot be changed in place. Create a revision with a reason, make the changes and approve the replacement. The earlier version remains in history.

Check the wording and explanations carefully. Locking prevents accidental edits in this demonstration; it is not production security.
